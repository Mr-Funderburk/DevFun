AP Computer Science Principles

AP CSP introduces you to the foundational concepts of computer science and challenges you to explore how computing and technology impact the world. Designed to broaden participation in technology, this course covers algorithms, programming, data analysis, internet infrastructure, and cybersecurity while preparing you for the AP Exam.
