Game Development I

Games aren't just played—they are carefully engineered systems of rules, feedback loops, and player psychology. In this course, you will transition from a game consumer to a game designer. You will start by deconstructing board games and building your own physical tabletop game, then shift into digital development using GameMaker Studio to bring 2D interactive worlds to life.
